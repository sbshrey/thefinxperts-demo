      How to update this review and what it cannot verify

      Upload a complete newer CAMS Active Statement to preview a fund refresh. A newer original mutual-fund CAS or broker report can update exact unique ISIN matches after confirmation; unmatched rows stay out and no holdings are removed by those partial refreshes. Active Statement additions stay unassigned and absent funds lose goal links only after confirmation. For a scheme with statement units, say “set NAV of holding 1 to ₹125.4321 as of YYYY-MM-DD” to preview a dated estimate after checking the exact scheme and unchanged units. For a stock with checked shares, say “set price of holding 1 to ₹125.43 as of YYYY-MM-DD” and check the listed security and current shares before confirming. To fix one row in chat, say “update value of NAME to ₹50,000 as of YYYY-MM-DD” or “remove NAME”. Use its number when names repeat. For an individual fund labelled Other, check its source and say classify holding 1 as Debt with the category you found. This is a factual review of supplied values; it does not verify current prices or give buy, sell, switch or allocation instructions. Drafts remain separate until you confirm them. Choose encrypted device saving or download a private review file to continue later.

      Save this review on this device

      Get a holdings report

      Choose one source you already use. Download the report from that provider, then return here and choose Upload. Check every draft before adding it to your review.

      Supported reports with up to 200 current holdings stay in this chat. If a broker export leaves type or date blank, check its numbered rows and answer the follow-up in chat; no row is added until you confirm.

      1. Mutual funds: Request a CAMS Active Statement with the email registered on your folios. It covers CAMS-serviced funds and arrives as an encrypted attachment using a password you choose on CAMS. For holdings across RTAs, request the CAMS + KFintech CAS. Choose Summary for balances or Detailed if you also need transactions; this review counts only current holdings that its browser preview can reconcile. Neither report guarantees complete demat holdings.
      2. Inside a fund: After confirming an exact scheme holding, download a dated monthly portfolio XLSX from Motilal Oswal's scheme portfolio page or Groww Mutual Fund's portfolio page, as appropriate, then upload it here. The reader supports checked Motilal Oswal scheme sheets and matching sheets in a Groww monthly workbook. It previews only schemes matching your confirmed fund names, at most five. Check each scheme and disclosure date before using it. A second supported sheet can show identified common listed shares. Dated fund values can give a partial estimate of visible issuer exposure; other assets and later trades remain outside that estimate. The sheet does not update your account value. The raw file stays in this tab; optional encrypted “Save here” can retain the checked weights on this device.
      3. Direct stocks: In Zerodha, use Kite’s User ID → Portfolio under Console → XLSX, or Console → Portfolio → Holdings → XLSX; see Zerodha’s instructions. In Groww, look under Reports for the Stock Holding Statement. For automatic preview, the CSV or XLSX must include a security name and current market value; a report showing quantities alone cannot establish your current value. If type or valuation date is missing, the chat asks you to check it. Unrecognized layouts add no holdings; you can describe a checked holding in chat instead.
      4. EPF: Sign in to the official EPFO Member Passbook portal with your own UAN credentials and download a member passbook PDF. A supported passbook becomes one draft after its final employee and employer balances reconcile; check the printed date, total and member account before confirming. Pension contribution, service-history PDFs and unverified layouts are excluded.
      5. NPS Tier I: Upload a Protean transaction-statement PDF to preview one dated balance when its scheme values reconcile with units and NAV. A password stays in this browser. Check the original total and every scheme before confirming; other statement layouts and Tier II may be unsupported. PPF, deposits and gold: Check a dated value on your own source, then describe one holding in chat. Bank statements and receipts or photos are not imported here. Check withdrawal or maturity terms before assigning savings to a goal.

      Use the password instructions that came with your own PDF; do not assume it uses your PAN. This public review reads the file in your browser. It does not connect to your broker or email.
