    Get a holdings report

    Choose one source you already use. Download the report from that provider, then return here and choose Upload. Check every draft before adding it to your review.

    1. Mutual funds: Request a CAMS Active Statement using your registered email. For a consolidated mutual-fund history, see the CAMS CAS page.
    2. Direct stocks: Download a dated XLSX holdings report from your broker. Zerodha shows the steps for Console and Kite. Other brokers may offer a similar CSV or XLSX export.
    3. NPS or EPF: These PDFs are not imported here yet. Check the latest balance and date on your own statement, then describe it in chat, such as “I own NPS Tier I worth ₹50,000”. The chat will ask for the date. Confirm the draft before using it, and check access terms before counting it toward a goal.

    Use the password instructions that came with your own PDF; do not assume it uses your PAN. This public review reads the file in your browser. It does not connect to your broker or email.
