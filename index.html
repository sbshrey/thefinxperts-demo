START WITH ONE SOURCE

How would you like to add holdings?

Choose what you have. You can add another source after the first import. Switching clears selected files and passwords.

Where do I get a report?
  1. Mutual funds, quick start: Request a CAMS Active Statement ↗ using the email registered with your folios. Save its encrypted PDF from your email, then choose “CAMS Active PDF or HTML” above. The browser preview uses individual schemes only when they reconcile to the fund-house totals; otherwise it shows those totals.
  2. Mutual funds across RTAs: CAMS offers a CAMS+KFintech CAS ↗ with summary or detailed options. Original CAS PDF preview appears here only when a private or local preview service is enabled; the public demo cannot read that PDF.
  3. Stocks: Get a holdings report with a current market value from your broker. For example, Zerodha explains its XLSX download ↗. Choose “Broker XLSX or CSV” and confirm the report date and columns.

Check for holdings missing from a source before adding another. Do not send a statement or its password to us by email.

STOCKS AND OTHER BROKER HOLDINGS

Have a broker holdings report?

Choose an XLSX or UTF-8 CSV holdings report and identify its name and current market value columns. Then classify every row before replacing the example. The report stays in this tab. Tradebooks, contract notes and purchase-cost columns are not current holdings values.

First XLSX worksheet only; up to 2 MB XLSX or 1 MB UTF-8 CSV and 200 positions. Confirm the report date and columns; no live prices are fetched.

Check the report columns before previewing holdings. Choose the header row if the report starts with a title.

CAMS ACTIVE STATEMENT

Have an Active Statement PDF or HTML attachment?

Preview the statement in this browser tab. The selected file and PDF password are not uploaded. Individual schemes appear only when every detected row reconciles to the fund-house summary; otherwise this preview shows fund-house totals. Scheme ISINs and fund overlap remain unknown.

PDF up to 15 MB; HTML up to 10 MB. This is for a CAMS Active Statement with an embedded or separate HTML attachment, not a standard CAS.

Open a row to correct its value, asset category or valuation date, or leave it out. CSV and scheme names can also be corrected; fund-house summary labels stay fixed. Supplied identifiers and statement scheme units are shown for checking; changing a name or asset clears its identifiers. Units are from the statement and are not a live NAV check. Check the source before changing an extracted figure.

For a detailed CAS only, an indicative annualized money-weighted return may appear on a row when opening units are zero and the supported cash flows reconcile. It is based on the statement period and its reported valuation, not a verified live price, benchmark comparison or forecast. A return disappears if you change the row's value, date, name or category.           AGE IS ONLY ONE INPUT

          Put the portfolio in context.

          Your goal date and ability to handle losses matter as much as the amount. Age is shown at the goal date, but it is not used to calculate an allocation or advice.

          Compare with what I could handle

          For this goal, enter either amount or both. What temporary fall could you cover from other resources without delaying the goal or essential spending? What fall could you tolerate without feeling forced to sell? These are your own estimates, not a formal risk profile.

          Compare the amounts with the illustrative loss above. A smaller illustrated loss does not prove an allocation is suitable; real losses and other obligations may differ.

          Compare with a mix I chose

          Enter your own percentages for this goal. They must total 100%. This compares asset categories in linked holdings; it does not suggest an allocation or a trade.

          Current shares use entered values and asset labels. Mixed or unclassified holdings stay in Other until you verify their category. Differences are percentage points, not amounts to buy or sell.

          Check the goal name and enter your age, goal cost and time horizon to turn on goal figures.
