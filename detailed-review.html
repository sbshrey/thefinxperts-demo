START WITH ONE SOURCE

How would you like to add holdings?

Choose what you have. You can add another source after the first import. Switching clears selected files and passwords.

Where do I get a report?
  1. Mutual funds, quick start: Request a CAMS Active Statement ↗ using the email registered with your folios. Save its encrypted PDF from your email, then choose “CAMS Active PDF or HTML” above. The browser preview uses individual schemes only when they reconcile to the fund-house totals; otherwise it shows those totals.
  2. Mutual funds across RTAs: CAMS offers a CAMS+KFintech CAS ↗ with summary or detailed options. Choose “Original CAS PDF” here to review larger statements, or use the guided chat for up to 30 rows. Unsupported layouts stop without adding holdings.
  3. Stocks: Get a holdings report with a current market value from your broker. For example, Zerodha explains its XLSX download ↗. Choose “Broker XLSX or CSV” and confirm the report date and columns. A supported NSDL/CDSL demat CAS can also be tried with “Original CAS PDF”; reconcile it with other reports before adding the same position twice.

Check for holdings missing from a source before adding another. Do not send a statement or its password to us by email.
