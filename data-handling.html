How your data is handled

This page describes the public browser-only review at thefinxperts.com. A separate private account service has not opened.

What happens in this browser

Manual entries, simple CSV files, broker XLSX or CSV files and CAMS Active Statement PDFs or HTML attachments are read in your browser tab. The guided and detailed reviews can also try original CAMS or KFintech mutual-fund CAS and NSDL or CDSL demat CAS PDFs in a disposable browser worker. The guided review can also read a supported EPFO member passbook in a disposable browser worker, checking its final employee and employer balances against the header. It uses only a one-way account code in the draft; the original member ID, PDF text and password are not retained. The report print date is a snapshot date, not proof that later EPF activity is included. A demat preview stops if its supported securities do not reconcile or it contains bonds or NPS that this review cannot yet represent. Demat equity type needs your confirmation. The password is used only to open that PDF. The worker sends only normalized holding drafts to the page; you must confirm them before they enter the review. Questions in “Ask this review” and the guided browser review are answered from these entries in the tab without an AI request. These inputs are not uploaded to TheFinxperts by the public review. They are not stored in browser session storage. The detailed review does not use browser local storage.

Entries remain in the tab while it is open. Opening Detailed review from a guided review with confirmed holdings, or returning from a personal detailed review to guided chat, copies the normalized holdings and goals once between two same-site browser tabs in memory. The link carries a random one-time token, not financial data; the destination removes it from the address after opening. Edits in either tab do not sync. Refreshing or closing a tab loses its review unless you chose to download a file or saved an encrypted copy with “Save here” in the guided review. After a successful statement preview, the file selection and password field are cleared. A rejected password leaves the selected PDF for another attempt; an unsupported unencrypted PDF is cleared. Your browser may retain temporary data in memory until it releases it or the tab closes.

Optional encrypted saving on this device

In the guided review, “Save here” encrypts the confirmed portfolio and goal details in this browser before putting the encrypted copy in local storage. It uses a passphrase you choose, PBKDF2 with SHA-256 and 310,000 iterations, and AES-256-GCM. Your passphrase stays in tab memory and is needed again after a refresh. Confirmed changes save automatically while the tab stays unlocked. Drafts, chat text, selected files and PDF passwords are not saved there.

“Forget here” deletes that browser copy. “Clear tab” also deletes it; downloaded files remain on your device. If you forget the passphrase, we cannot recover the browser copy. Browser storage may be cleared by your browser, and someone with access to an unlocked tab can read the review. Keep a private file backup if you need a second copy. The detailed review can receive the currently open guided review, and the guided chat can receive the currently open detailed review, when you choose their links. Neither page unlocks or reads the encrypted device copy on its own.

Files you choose to download

The readable summary and restorable JSON contain your holdings, values, goals, the original entry route and latest broker valuation route when applicable, any imported scheme category and units, any user-entered NAV or stock-price estimate with its earlier value and dates, any entered stock share count, any fund expense ratios and dates, and any reserve totals you enter. A download saves a copy on your device under your browser's download settings. Keep it private and delete it from your device when you no longer need it. Clearing the page does not delete a file you downloaded.

Network requests and other services

Your browser requests the review's page, scripts, styles and fonts from GitHub Pages. The public pages use a content policy that limits scripts and network requests to this site; it does not replace checking the domain and HTTPS before opening a statement. The review code does not request Google Fonts or another third-party service while you review holdings. It does not put holdings, questions or statement contents in page requests. There is no analytics tracker or advertising code in the public review.

The public review has no Gmail connection, account saving or live market feed. The original CAS option in the guided review parses in the browser and makes no PDF upload request. Its scripts come from this same site. Its automatic feature checks are GET requests to the same site; they do not include a portfolio or PDF.

Before a private account service opens

A separate Auth0 and PostgreSQL service is planned. If enabled, signing in and explicitly saving a review will involve those providers and a saved copy of normalized holdings, goals and any optional reserve totals. Its optional signed-in CAS preview would send a PDF and password to that private service for one request; the public guided CAS preview stays in the browser. The private service needs its own final notice covering providers, data location, retention, deletion and a contact route before real users are invited.
